2.4 · Behavioural factor work

Behavioural factor work

Acceleration on specific queries — with the risks stated up front and an exit plan.

A tool for narrow cases: nudging pages that already sit close to the top. We are straightforward about it — this is a grey area, search engines do not sanction it, and we only take it on when the risk is understood and discussed with you.

  • Narrow use only: queries where the page already ranks in the top 20
  • Never a substitute for proper optimization — only an addition to it
  • Risks stated before we start: what happens if it stops, and if penalties hit
  • Continuous ranking and traffic monitoring to catch deviation early
  • We stop at your first word and at the first warning sign

In detail · AI integration

How we put AI into a business

The three areas above — the assistant, operations and customer conversations — are assembled from the same building blocks. Below is what exactly we connect, and to what. Scope and cost are priced to your specific situation, by agreement.

What we actually connect

by agreement

The same approach, applied more broadly: we look at your processes and work out where AI genuinely saves time and money — and where it isn't needed. Not an off-the-shelf product — we build around your task.

AI assistants and chatbots

Support and sales bots, internal helpers for your team — they take load off your managers and answer customers without delay. For example: a first-line bot handles routine questions about order status, delivery and terms, and only hands a manager what actually needs a person. The team works the hard cases instead of the whole inbox.

Automating the routine

Requests, documents, reports, commercial offers — the work that eats hours on repetitive steps. For example: an incoming request from email or a form is parsed into fields on its own, pulls the data it needs, and turns into a ready offer in your template. The manager just reviews and sends — minutes instead of an hour of manual work.

Integrations with your systems

CRM, ERP, 1C, your site, databases, internal services — we connect directly, without replacing what already works. For example: customer and order history from your CRM becomes available to the assistant, so it answers with the specific deal in mind rather than in generic phrases. Your systems stay where they are — we add a layer that ties them together.

Analytics and working with data

We put scattered data in order and make it searchable, with short summaries instead of manually combing through tables and documents. For example: rather than scrolling through dozens of files and chats, an employee asks a question in plain words and gets an answer with a link to the source in your knowledge base. Finding what you need takes seconds, not half a day.

We don't bend your task to fit a ready-made product — we build the solution around your processes, in whatever format suits you and without replacing what already works. We start with a short review: we show honestly where the return is real, and where it's too early to invest.
