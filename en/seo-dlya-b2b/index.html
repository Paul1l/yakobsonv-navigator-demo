B2B and Manufacturing SEO: Growing Your Industrial Catalog

A large equipment catalog, yet traffic stays cold and enquiries are few — the site works like a business card. Some agencies can't handle scale across tens of thousands of pages; others spin up thin duplicate pages that drag the whole site down in rankings. We specialise in industrial B2B catalogs: we expand coverage at scale, fix indexing, and set up analytics so warm enquiries come in from your commercial pages. No promises of "top rankings in a month" — just an honest process.

What you get

  1. 01

    Full-catalog technical audit

    A complete crawl of every page, not a sample: we surface broken links, duplicates, wrong canonical URLs and pages accidentally dropped from the index. You get a prioritised list of the issues holding the whole site back — and we fix them directly.

  2. 02

    Page creation at scale

    Thousands of catalog cards and landing pages built to one proven structure — directly on your Bitrix, WordPress, Tilda or mottor. The catalog opens up across hundreds of target queries, with no page-by-page hand editing and no drop in quality.

  3. 03

    Price and stars right in the results

    Structured markup for your organization, products and services, price and rating, and question-and-answer blocks. This is a bid for rich snippets — your result stands out over competitors, lifting click-through on commercial queries.

  4. 04

    Indexing recovery and protection

    We track not "how many we created" but "how many are actually in search." On one industrial project, after a server failure we brought pages back into the Yandex index — excluded pages fell 10x (1,740 → 165). Your pages earn traffic instead of sitting dead.

  5. 05

    A private 24/7 project dashboard

    Live numbers on traffic, positions, indexing and the health of every site, plus a log of every edit. At any moment you can see what's done and what it delivered — instead of waiting for a monthly PDF.

  6. 06

    Warm leads, not just visits

    End-to-end analytics on forms, calls and email, with the source passed straight into the enquiry message. You see which pages and queries bring enquiries — and the budget goes into strengthening exactly what already brings clients.

How we work

We work through a direct, safe connection to your CMS: every edit is made with a backup snapshot taken beforehand and the option to roll it back. Quality is checked at every step, before anything goes live. All progress is visible in the private dashboard 24/7 — traffic, positions, indexing, the state of each site and the work log in real time. Transparent, no black box.

Frequently asked

Can you handle our volume — tens of thousands of pages across different platforms? +

Yes. On one project we run 40,000+ pages across four sites on four different CMSs (Bitrix, Bitrix24 and a static generator) under a single tool, with 33,800+ of them in the Yandex index. Each site connects as its own module with its own adapter, and all the work happens in one dashboard. Send us your volume and stack — in 10 minutes we'll tell you whether it fits.

How much does it cost, and what drives the price? +

The price depends on catalog size, the number of sites and engines, the state of indexing, and what you need first — audit, mass pages, markup, or all of it. After a free review of your URL we give a clear range and a sensible starting point so the budget pays back. No forced "everything at once" packages.

Do you guarantee top rankings? +

No — and no honest specialist does. Scale gives you coverage and a foundation; sustained position growth shows up after re-indexing, over time, not in the first month. We show real numbers, not an invented percentage. For context: on one industrial catalog we reached 99% of the core in Yandex's top-10 (497 of 500 queries over 90 days) — but that's the result of the work, not a promise made upfront.

Do you work with our CMS? We're on Bitrix with some custom parts. +

Yes. Bitrix, Bitrix24, WordPress, Tilda, mottor, static generators. Each platform gets its own adapter; we make edits directly and safely, with backup snapshots taken first. Multiple sites on different engines get consolidated into a single setup with one shared dashboard.

How many leads will you bring? +

No one can honestly name an exact number in advance — it depends on your niche, demand and competition. Our job is to widen the catalog's coverage and set up analytics so it's clear which pages generate enquiries, then steadily strengthen those. Warm leads come from commercial pages that reach the top for their target queries.

We're B2B — competitors shouldn't know we hired you. +

NDA by default. Most of our projects are B2B industrial, where competitors care even about the fact that you have a contractor at all. We never publish client names or domains; case studies show only anonymised, industry-level numbers. We'll walk you through a detailed case from your industry on a call under NDA.

Send us the URL of your catalog — within 24 hours we'll come back with a short review: titles, speed, indexing, markup and competitors in the results. We'll tell you straight whether we can handle your volume and how we'd grow it. No obligation.
